Sheet

Examples for the ss-c-sheet module.

A panel that slides in from the bottom (default), left or right edge of its positioned parent, or of the viewport with --fixed. While closed only its __handle shows; the body is hidden from keyboard and screen readers. State is data-state="open" or "closed". SheetManager toggles it from the handle or any data-ss-sheet-toggle button, keeps aria-expanded in sync, opens and closes with the arrow keys on the handle, and closes on Escape. Size tokens: --ss-sheet-size, --ss-sheet-peek.

Bottom sheet

Buildings, roads and water.

HTML 
<div style="position: relative; height: 300px; overflow: hidden; background: var(--ss-color-surface-2);">
            <section class="ss-c-sheet" id="demo-sheet-bottom" data-state="closed" aria-label="Layers">
                <button type="button" class="ss-c-sheet__handle" aria-controls="demo-sheet-bottom"
                        aria-expanded="false">Layers</button>
                <div class="ss-c-sheet__body">
                    <p style="margin: 0">Buildings, roads and water.</p>
                </div>
            </section>
        </div>

Open

Wall 0042 · IfcWallStandardCase · 3.20 m

HTML 
<div style="position: relative; height: 300px; overflow: hidden; background: var(--ss-color-surface-2);">
            <section class="ss-c-sheet" id="demo-sheet-open" data-state="open" aria-label="Selection"
                     style="--ss-sheet-size: 60%">
                <button type="button" class="ss-c-sheet__handle" aria-controls="demo-sheet-open"
                        aria-expanded="true">Selection</button>
                <div class="ss-c-sheet__body">
                    <p style="margin: 0">Wall 0042 &middot; IfcWallStandardCase &middot; 3.20 m</p>
                </div>
            </section>
        </div>

Sheet on small screens, dock on large

--dock is a bottom sheet below md and a plain, always-open column from md up (the handle hides). Here it is the right column of a flex row.
